Manya Bansal

I’m part of the founding team at humans&, where I lead the user modeling team with Alexis Ross.

Before humans&, I was a Ph.D. student at MIT, where I worked with Saman Amarasinghe and Jonathan Ragan-Kelley on programming languages for high performance computing. My research focused on helping programmers write fast, low-level GPU code that is still modular, reusable, and correct. I believe programming systems should give people precise control over hardware while making that control easier to compose and trust.

I received my B.S. in pure mathematics from Stanford in 2023. There, I was lucky to work with Fredrik Kjolstad and Dawson Engler, who let me tinker and build many, many small things.
